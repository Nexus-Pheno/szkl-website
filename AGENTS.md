# SZKL Website Working Rules

## Bilingual content QA

- Treat every user-visible content change as an English and Chinese change. Update both language variants together, normally in `src/i18n.ts`.
- Before publishing, compare the English and Chinese versions for meaning, hierarchy, links, labels, and calls to action. Use natural Chinese rather than a rigid word-for-word translation.
- Test the language toggle and every changed section on both desktop and mobile. Check wrapping, overflow, navigation, buttons, and accessibility labels in each language.
- Do not publish a content change until the Chinese iteration has been reviewed and its parity with the English iteration is confirmed.
- Preserve approved brand wording that intentionally remains in English. In particular, do not create or publish a Chinese translation of the approved Pulse slogan lockup until Michael approves one.
- In the final handoff for any content change, explicitly state that both English and Chinese versions were checked.
